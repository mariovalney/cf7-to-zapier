CF7 to Webhook

Send Contact Form 7 submissions to Zapier or any webhook as JSON. Free and open source.

What it does

  • Any webhook

    One or more URLs per form, with GET, POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE.

  • Your own body and headers

    Build the request body and headers with the form fields, as in Contact Form 7 mail.

  • Templates

    Start from templates made by the community, such as Slack.

  • Files

    Send uploads as links or as base64 content, and keep them in the media library.

  • Error notifications

    Get an email with the request and the response when a webhook fails.

  • Multi-step forms

    Works with Contact Form 7 Multi-Step Forms and sends the data of every step.

Get started

  1. Install and activate Contact Form 7 and CF7 to Webhook.
  2. Edit a form and open the Webhook tab.
  3. Check Send to Webhook, paste your webhook URL and save.
