Get the free text of checkboxes and radios

The free_text option of Contact Form 7 adds a text box to the last option of a checkbox or radio field:

[checkbox your-options "Option 1" "Option 2" "Other" free_text]

When the visitor checks "Other" and types a text, the plugin sends the text in place of "Other": ["Option 1", "typed text"] for a checkbox and "typed text" for a radio. Nothing else is needed.
