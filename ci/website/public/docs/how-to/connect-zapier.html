Documentation / How-to

Connect a form to Zapier

Zapier receives the form data through a webhook trigger. You need a Zapier account and a form already working in Contact Form 7.

Create the trigger in Zapier

  1. Create a Zap and choose Webhooks by Zapier as the trigger, with the Catch Hook event.
  2. Copy the webhook URL that Zapier shows. It starts with https://hooks.zapier.com/hooks/catch/.

Connect the form

  1. In WordPress, edit the form and open the Webhook tab.
  2. Check Send to Webhook, paste the URL in Webhook URL and save.
  3. Submit the form on your site with test data.

While the webhook is on, Contact Form 7 does not send its email for this form. To keep the email, check Send CF7 mail as usually.

Finish the Zap

Back in Zapier, test the trigger. It shows the submission with one key per field, named after the field: [text* your-name] arrives as your-name. Use those keys in the next steps of the Zap.

To send other keys, see Rename the fields sent to the webhook. If nothing arrives, see Troubleshoot missing data.
