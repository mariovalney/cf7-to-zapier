Templates

Since 4.0.0, templates fill Headers and Body for a service, such as Slack. Pick one in Template, under Settings in the webhook panel.

What a template changes

After you confirm, the template replaces the body, replaces or clears the headers and clears Special Mail Tags. Where the template has __VALUES__, the plugin writes the fields of your form. When the template links to documentation, the plugin offers to open it.

Nothing is saved until you save the form, and you can still edit the result. See Create a custom body.

Where templates come from

The list is the file https://cf7-to-webhook.valney.dev/templates.json. Your browser loads it when you open the form editor, so new templates show up without updating the plugin.
